Our story

We sit at 88 Lê Hữu Trác Street — named after one of Vietnam's greatest physicians, who left the imperial court for a forest hut and spent thirty years writing a 66-volume encyclopedia of medicine. He gave himself the pen name Lãn Ông — the Lazy Old Man. He didn't stop. He just stopped rushing — and found his way back to himself.
